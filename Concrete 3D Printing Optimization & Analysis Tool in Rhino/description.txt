Project description *
Developed a custom Grasshopper plugin suite (10+ C# .NET modules) for Rhino 7 to analyze and optimize the 3D printability of irregular concrete masses. Commissioned for a university architectural research project, the tool automates complex internal infill geometries, including calculated area offsets and multi-core optimized (Parallel.For) zigzag routing. It generates 3D mesh ribbons representing print beads and features an advanced analyzer that visualizes overfill and underfill zones via color-coded maps, ensuring structural viability before physical printing.
